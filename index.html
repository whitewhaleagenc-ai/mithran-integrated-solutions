The problem

Four vendors, four handovers, four places to lose money.

Solar sites need land. Land deals need structuring. Materials need moving. What is left over needs sorting and recovery. Most projects hand each step to a different firm — and the money leaks at the handovers.

Contract 01

A solar contractor with its own scope, timeline and price.

Contract 02

A land agent who never sees the system design.

Contract 03

A freight operator booked after the schedule has slipped.

Contract 04

A scrap buyer who quotes only after the work is done.

A separate solar contractor, land agent, freight operator and scrap buyer means four contracts, four timelines and nobody accountable for the gaps between them. The delays and the shortfalls almost always live in those gaps.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers.

Can't find yours? Message us on WhatsApp and we will reply directly.

What does Mithran Integrated Solutions do?

Mithran Integrated Solutions runs four connected lines of work: industrial solar and land, business consulting, raw-material transport, and industrial scrap recovery. They are offered as one chain so that a project does not lose time or money at the handovers between separate vendors.

Where is Mithran Integrated Solutions based?

Mithran Integrated Solutions is based in Madurai, Tamil Nadu, India.

Can I hire you for only one of the four services?

Yes. Each link stands on its own — you can take solar and land, consulting, transport or scrap recovery separately, or the whole chain. We will say honestly which parts we should run and which you already have covered.

What kind of solar work do you do?

Industrial solar: site scoping, system sizing, solar framing and the paperwork behind it. We also source and broker the land itself, both solar-ready and agricultural parcels.

Do you buy and sell land?

Yes. We source and broker land parcels, including solar-ready sites and agricultural land, as part of the solar and land vertical.

What do you transport?

Bulk raw materials. We handle fleet and logistics coordination for raw-material freight, including route planning aimed at cutting fuel spend and turnaround time.

Do you buy industrial scrap?

We handle metal sorting and scrap recovery, routing industrial byproduct back into working capital through recycling pipelines and sustainable waste handling.
